2. What are the main causes of prediction collapse in wearable HAR under domain shift?
3. How much distribution mismatch in accelerometer/gyroscope data is enough to break a trained HAR model?

6. What happens when training and production data come from different user populations?
7. How can we quantify train–test domain gap for wearable sensor time series?
8. Which feature shifts are most harmful in HAR: mean shift, variance shift, orientation shift, or temporal-pattern shift?
9. Can high-confidence wrong predictions be used as a signature of domain shift?
10. How should real-world out-of-distribution human motion be detected in HAR?

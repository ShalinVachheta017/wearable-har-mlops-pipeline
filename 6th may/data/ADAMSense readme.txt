The dataset has following fields:
Time_p: Time stamp of smartphone at pocket position	
Ax_p: Accelerometer (x) of smartphone at pocket position	
Ay_p: Accelerometer (y) of smartphone at pocket position		
Az_p: Accelerometer (z) of smartphone at pocket position		
Gx_p: Gyroscope (x) of smartphone at pocket position			
Gy_p: Gyroscope (y) of smartphone at pocket position				
Gz_p: Gyroscope (z) of smartphone at pocket position				
Mx_p: Magnetometer (x) of smartphone at pocket position						
My_p: Magnetometer (y) of smartphone at pocket position							
Mz_p: Magnetometer (z) of smartphone at pocket position							
GPS1_p	
GPS2_p	
PocketDev: Device used at pocket position	
Time_w: Time stamp of IMU at wrist position	
Ax_w: Accelerometer (x) of IMU at wrist position		
Ay_w: Accelerometer (y) of IMU at wrist position			
Az_w: Accelerometer (z) of IMU at wrist position			
Gx_w: Gyroscope (x) of IMU at wrist position				
Gy_w: Gyroscope (y) of IMU at wrist position					
Gz_w: Gyroscope (z) of IMU at wrist position					
Mx_w: Magnetometer (x) of IMU at wrist position					
My_w: Magnetometer (y) of IMU at wrist position						
Mz_w: Magnetometer (z) of IMU at wrist position						
WristDev: Device used at wrist position
User: User ID
Activity: (Class) Activity Labels	

The datset has total 709583 samples for 11 activities of Ear_rubbing, Forehead_rubbing, Hair_pulling, Hand_scratching, Hand_tapping, Knuckles_cracking, Nail_biting, Nape_rubbing, Smoking, 
Sitting, and Standing collected by 10 participants.
